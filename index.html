THE METHOD

A small edit.
A separate test.

Observe a failure, investigate its mechanism, and modify the policy. Development feedback guides the edit; inaccessible held-out layouts test the frozen result.

—charged development episodes
—held-out layouts

Infrastructure refunds mean the charged budget is not an exact count of attempted episodes. Source inspection is permitted; these experiments do not certify independent discovery.

What counts as an edit?

Hand-written changes and closed-form fits from forward-pass data are allowed. Gradient-based fitting, fine-tuning, and LoRA are not. Gradients may be used for diagnosis, not to optimize the edit.

Diagnostic interventions are not the final policy: the submitted change must be saved in the weights.

What does the comparison ask?

Python + curated toolkit versus Python alone: both agents can inspect model internals, use hooks and replay, write their own mechanistic analyses, and save weight edits. The full-tool agent is a superset: it can use the packaged tools, write custom Python, or combine both.

Python-only removes ready-made analyses, not interpretability. Black-box agents also deliver weight edits, using behavioral feedback and action-bias editing. This is a tool-suite ablation, not a no-weight-edit baseline.

Strong Python-only results support the broader weight-editing approach, not the necessity or superiority of the curated toolkit. Different counts, controls, and selected cohorts prevent a simple causal ranking.
